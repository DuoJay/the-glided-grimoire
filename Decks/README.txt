The Gilded Grimoire | Offline edition

1. Double-click the ZIP in Finder to extract the whole folder.
2. Open The Gilded Grimoire, then double-click index.html.
   Use a current version of Safari, Chrome, or Firefox.

Draw both cards at the table, select the deck and the two cards, and read the
outcome. Use Record physical draw to add it to the history.

To move campaign history and DM edits from the hosted site, turn on DM mode,
open Card library, and choose Export campaign backup. In the offline edition,
open the same controls and choose Import backup. The same backup can also
move your campaign between browsers or folders.

Your browser manages saved edits and history for this local copy. Keep the
folder in one location and export a campaign backup before moving it or
switching browsers. If your browser reports that storage is unavailable,
export a backup before closing the page and import it next time.

Included rulebook pages open offline. Optional D&D Beyond links need an
internet connection. The offline edition uses local system fonts.
